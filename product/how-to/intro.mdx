Most people land here because C1 sent them a notification: a request to approve, a review to complete, or a reminder to connect an AI tool. Find your task below, or work through the quickstart if this is your first time signing in.

<Tip>
Setting up C1 for your organization — policies, connectors, access profiles — instead of using it day to day? Go to [Administer C1](/product/intro).
</Tip>

## First time in C1?

If a colleague or an automated notification sent you here, these three steps cover the whole path from sign-in to resolution.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to your company's C1 URL (for example, `acme.conductor.one`) and sign in with your company's SSO credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the request or task from your notification">
    C1 notifies you by email, Slack, or Microsoft Teams when a request or task needs your attention. Open the link in that notification, or check the **Requests** or **Tasks** page in C1 directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the request or task">
    Follow the prompts C1 shows you for that specific request or task — the steps differ depending on whether you're requesting access, approving a request, or completing a review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

C1 tracks the status of your request or task and notifies you when it changes.

## Frequently asked questions about using C1

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a request and a review?">
    A request is something you ask for, like access to an app. A review is something you're asked to do, like confirming that a colleague's access is still appropriate as part of a [user access review campaign](/product/how-to/review-tasks). Both can show up as tasks that need your attention.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see an app I need access to?">
    C1 admins control which apps and permissions you can see and request, based on your department or role. See [How to request new access](/product/how-to/create-requests) for more on how this works.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my access change without me requesting it?">
    Someone with the right permissions — an approver, an admin, or an automated policy — can grant or revoke access on your behalf. Check [How to complete access change tasks](/product/how-to/access-change-tasks) if you're the one responsible for making that change for someone else.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I make sure I see task notifications?">
    C1 notifies you by email, Slack, or Microsoft Teams. Confirm your notification preferences on the [notification settings page](/product/admin/notifications-user-settings).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
