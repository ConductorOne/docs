Control who can use an Edge Gateway by granting its two entitlements. Until someone holds them, the Edge rejects their requests, and callers see messages such as "You don't have access to an Edge."

## Edge Gateway entitlements

Each Edge has two entitlements on its parent application:

| Entitlement | Scope | Lets the caller |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **List models** | `inference.models.read` | See which models the Edge serves (`GET /v1/models`) |
| **Call models** | `inference.invoke` | Send requests to the Edge's models |

Grant both to most callers. **Call models** doesn't include **List models**, and many tools list models before calling one. Workload callers need both.

## Grant access to people or service principals

Grant access from the Edge's **Grants** tab.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the Edge: **Apps** > *your app* > **Edge** > *your Edge*.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the **Grants** tab.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **List models** or **Call models**, click **Grant access**.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose where to pick from: **All people**, or **User accounts in this app**.
</Step>
<Step>
Select one or more people, or service principals if the Edge accepts them.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Grant access**.
</Step>
<Step>
Repeat for the other entitlement.
</Step>
</Steps>

  <img src="/images/product/assets/edge-grant-access.png" alt="The Grant access: Call models dialog with a note that every route accepts people, the All people and User accounts in this app options, and a person picker."/>
</Frame>

Each grantee appears under the entitlement with a **Manual** chip. They can call the Edge the next time their client requests a token. To revoke access, click **Remove** next to the grantee.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product/assets/edge-grants.png" alt="The Grants tab listing people granted List models and Call models, each with Person and Manual chips and a Remove action."/>
</Frame>

<Note>
Who you can grant depends on **Who can call** on the Edge's **Overview** tab. When every route accepts people, you can grant people but not service principals. When no route accepts people, you can grant only service principals. Grants go to C1 identities, so app accounts that aren't linked to an identity can't receive them.
</Note>

## Optional: Grant access to every account of the app automatically

To give everyone with an account in the parent app access, turn on automatic grants for an entitlement: on the **Grants** tab, under **List models** or **Call models**, click **Populate with all accounts of** *app*.

Every account of the app now gets the entitlement, and C1 updates the list after each sync. This includes the app's service accounts, not only people. If you turn automatic grants off, C1 removes the access it granted automatically after the next sync.

<Note>
Automatic grants aren't available for an Edge on the built-in C1 app. Grant access manually instead, or create the Edge on another application.
</Note>

## What callers see after you grant access

Once a person holds the entitlements:

- C1 Multipass lists the Edge, and its **Route AI traffic through Edge** setting can use it. See [Route other tools through C1 Multipass](/product/admin/connect-edge-gateway-multipass).
- The Edge appears under **Model endpoints** in their **AI & API** > **Inference API keys** page when **Allow long-lived API keys** is on. This setting isn't needed for the Multipass key helper. See [Use long-lived API keys](/product/admin/inference-api-keys).
