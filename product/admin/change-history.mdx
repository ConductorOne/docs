## View an object's change history

Many configuration objects in C1 — connectors, policies, entitlement routing rules, SSO and branding settings, AI Gateway spend controls, and more — show a **Change history** icon on their detail page.

Click it to open a timeline of every change made to that object: who made it, when, and exactly what changed, with a clear before-and-after for each affected field.
