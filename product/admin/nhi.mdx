Non-human identities don't go through the onboarding and offboarding events that keep a human identity list accurate, which makes them easy to lose track of over time. The **Identities & NHI** dashboard gives you one place to see every service account, secret, and other non-human identity C1 has discovered across your connected sources.

<Note>
C1's NHI terminology and classification are actively evolving. This page reflects current product behavior and will be updated as classification changes.
</Note>

## What counts as an NHI

A non-human identity (NHI) is an account or credential used by a system rather than a person — for example, a [service account](/product/glossary#service-account), a [service principal](/product/glossary#service-principal), or a [system account](/product/glossary#system-account). See the [glossary](/product/glossary) for definitions of each NHI subtype C1 recognizes.

## Find NHIs across your environment

Navigate to **Identity security** > **Identities & NHI** to view the dashboard.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product/assets/nhi-identities-table.png" alt="The Identities & NHI dashboard's NHI tab, listing non-human identities grouped by connected app, with columns for owner, type, subtype, and findings." />
</Frame>

The dashboard has a tab for each identity type — **Human users**, **Service**, **Secrets**, and **Agents** — plus a combined **NHI** tab that rolls up every non-human identity across those types. Each tab lists identities grouped by the connected app they came from, with:

- **Type and subtype** — the resource's classification, such as an Entra app registration or an AWS assumable role, alongside a technical subtype identifier.
- **Owner** — who owns the identity, if anyone. Unowned NHIs are one of the most common gaps this dashboard surfaces.
- **Findings** — any open [findings](/product/admin/findings) tied to the identity, such as an unowned non-human identity.

Search by name, or filter by app, owner, type, or **Has findings** to narrow the list down.

Click an identity's finding to open its detail view, with the same evidence, remediation guidance, and activity trail described in [Findings](/product/admin/findings#work-with-a-finding).

</Frame>

## Current scope

C1 surfaces non-human identities across connected sources today. Ownership assignment, policy inclusion, and lifecycle management for NHIs aren't available yet.
