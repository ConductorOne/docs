Have an issue with C1, a bug to report, or a feature to request? You can reach the C1 support team through several channels. Each ticket is stored in your C1 support portal (via Pylon) so you can track its status in one place.

## Ways to submit a ticket

Choose whichever channel fits how your team already works with C1.

### Email

Send an email to [support@c1.ai](mailto:support@c1.ai). A ticket is created automatically, and replies to the thread are added to it.

### Support portal

Submit and track tickets directly in your dedicated support portal. If you don't have a portal link yet, contact [support@c1.ai](mailto:support@c1.ai) and we'll get it set up for you. If you have a Slack channel, the portal link is in the **Bookmarks** folder.

### Slack

If your team has a shared Slack channel with C1, post your question or issue in the channel, then react to the message with the 🎫 (ticket) emoji. This turns the message into a ticket, and the thread stays connected to it.

### Microsoft Teams

If your team has a shared Teams channel with C1, post your message in the channel and react with the 🎫 (ticket) emoji to create a ticket.

## Support levels

Your support level is set in your C1 agreement, and determines which support channels are available to you, whether you have a named customer success manager (CSM), and your P0 coverage.

| | Basic | Business | Enterprise | Mission critical |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Support channels | Email, support portal (Slack available as an add-on) | Email, support portal, Slack | Email, support portal, Slack or Teams | Email, support portal, Slack or Teams |
| Customer success manager | Not included | Not included | Named CSM | Named strategic CSM |
| P0 coverage | Business hours | Business hours | 24x7 on-call | 24x7 on-call |

Business hours are 6:30 AM to 8:30 PM ET, Monday through Friday.

<Note>We're introducing support levels for all customers, and some existing agreements don't include one yet. If yours doesn't, your CSM will work with you to confirm the level that applies to your account. In the meantime, contact [support@c1.ai](mailto:support@c1.ai) with any questions.</Note>

## Setting ticket priority

You can set a priority when you submit a ticket. Priority tells us how urgently you need help and sets the response time target for your ticket.

| Priority | When to use it |
|---|---|
| P0: Critical | C1 is down or a core function is unavailable with no workaround, and your business is blocked. |
| P1: High | A core function is significantly degraded or failing for many users, with no reasonable workaround. |
| P2: Medium | A feature isn't working as expected, but a workaround exists or the impact is limited. |
| P3: Low | General questions, configuration help, minor bugs, and feature requests. |

Our support team may adjust a ticket's priority if it doesn't match these definitions, and we'll let you know if we do.

<Warning>For **Enterprise** and **Mission critical** customers, P0 tickets page our on-call team around the clock, including overnight. Reserve P0 for critical issues that are blocking your business.</Warning>

### Response time targets

Response time is measured from when a ticket is created to the first response from our support team.

| Priority | Basic | Business | Enterprise | Mission critical |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | 1 business day | 2 business hours | 30 minutes | 15 minutes |
| P1 | 1 business day | 4 business hours | 1 business hour | 1 business hour |
| P2 | 1 business day | 1 business day | 4 business hours | 4 business hours |
| P3 | 2 business days | 2 business days | 1 business day | 1 business day |

Enterprise and Mission critical P0 targets apply 24x7. All other targets are measured in business hours.

## Ticket request type

After you submit a ticket, it's tagged with a request type so it's routed to the right people.

## Changing a ticket's priority or request type

You can update a ticket's priority and request type at any time if the situation changes or the original tag doesn't fit. The C1 support team also reserves the right to adjust the type or priority, within reason.
